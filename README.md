# BTAM Assessment Analyzer Reader

A GitHub Pages-ready demo tool for translating completed BTAM assessment forms and documentation into professional plain-language interpretation, missing facts, immediate management actions, supportive intervention direction, follow-up ownership, and proof-of-care documentation.

## Purpose

This tool helps school officials follow a simple professional flow:

Concern → Facts → Themes → Unknowns → Action → Support → Follow-up → Human Review

## Professional Plain-Language Interpretation

The tool explains completed assessment information in clear, professional language so a school official can quickly understand:

- what happened
- why it matters
- what facts are known
- what facts are missing
- what support may be needed
- what action happens next
- who owns follow-up
- when the team checks back

## What This Tool Does

- Accepts pasted assessment notes or uploaded test files
- Supports `.txt`, `.md`, `.csv`, and `.json` files in the public demo
- Detects common concern themes
- Converts complex assessment language into professional plain English
- Lists missing facts and critical questions
- Produces immediate management action steps
- Suggests supportive intervention direction
- Creates a follow-up owner/date section
- Produces a proof-of-care statement
- Allows copy, print, and text download

## What This Tool Does Not Do

This tool does not diagnose, predict violence, replace a BTAM team, replace law enforcement, replace mental-health judgment, or guarantee legal/regulatory compliance.

## Important Safety Notice

Use demo or training data only. Do not enter real student, employee, medical, disciplinary, threat, or personally identifiable information into a public GitHub Pages demo.
