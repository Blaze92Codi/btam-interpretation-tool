Proof-of-Care Checklist

  • Concern was documented using observable facts.
  • Unknowns were identified instead of guessed.
  • Team review or escalation path was identified.
  • Supportive measures were considered.
  • Safety steps were separated from discipline/punishment.
  • Follow-up owner and date were assigned.
  • No final decision was made solely by software or AI.
