# BTAM Analyzer Reader Knowledge Guide

## Core belief

Assessment is not a score. Assessment is the process of turning concern into clarity, clarity into action, and action into monitored support.

## End-user flow

1. What happened?
2. Who reported it?
3. What facts are known?
4. What behavior changed?
5. What themes are present?
6. What facts are missing?
7. What should happen today?
8. What support should be offered?
9. Who owns follow-up?
10. When does the team check again?

## Interpretation levels

Routine means there is not enough concerning information yet, but the concern should still be documented.

Low means there is a concern, but it appears stable and manageable with support and monitoring.

Moderate means the team should review promptly because multiple concern themes or stressors are present.

High means same-day review is needed because threat, target, escalation, planning, or access concerns may be present.

Critical means normal workflow stops and emergency/safety procedures should be followed according to policy.

## Proof-of-care logic

Concern received → facts organized → unknowns identified → concern interpreted → action assigned → support considered → follow-up scheduled → human review required.
